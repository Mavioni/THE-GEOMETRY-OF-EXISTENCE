## Where it stops working

*The Reversal, the place where the teaching stops working:*

> Peace is not passivity before injustice. Some anger is righteous, and some situations demand a fierce answer: when someone is being harmed, when a line must be held. The aim is not to feel nothing. It is to act from judgment rather than from the surge. And peace is never bought by abandoning someone who needs defending.

## To carry

*The Song:*

*Write it hot, and write it all,*<br>
*And fold it, and lay it by;*<br>
*The ink will cool in the drawer by dawn*<br>
*Though the heart is running high.*<br>
*For the word that is sent in the heat of the hour*<br>
*Is a stone that you cannot recall;*<br>
*And the letter that sleeps in the drawer for a night*<br>
*Is the one that was best of all.*
