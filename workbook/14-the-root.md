## Where it stops working

*The Reversal, the place where the teaching stops working:*

> “Honour your parents” has been used to silence people who were harmed by them. Honour never requires you to accept abuse, to stay in danger, or to keep secrets that shelter harm. Protecting yourself is not dishonour. Sometimes the most honourable thing is distance.

## To carry

*The Song:*

*The oak is as deep as the oak is high,*<br>
*Though the half of it none may see;*<br>
*And the root that holds in the winter gale*<br>
*Was a gift to the growing tree.*<br>
*So give your thanks to the hidden root,*<br>
*And grow as the root would ask;*<br>
*And leave in the earth for the ones to come*<br>
*A root that is fit for the task.*
