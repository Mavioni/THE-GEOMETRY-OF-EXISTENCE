## The Practice

**Daily.** Each morning ask: what good shall I do today? Each night ask: what good have I done?

## Where it stops working

*The Reversal, the place where the teaching stops working:*

> A rite can go hollow: performed by habit while the heart is elsewhere. If your practice has gone dead, change its form rather than abandoning its purpose. And never let your rite become a reason to judge others for not keeping it.

## To carry

*The Song:*

*The sun comes up at the same small hour,*<br>
*But never in quite the same place;*<br>
*It wanders a figure of eight through the year,*<br>
*And it keeps to that figure with grace.*<br>
*So keep your hour, though the days may drift,*<br>
*And mark them, one by one;*<br>
*And the shape you will trace by the end of the year*<br>
*Will be yours, as the eight is the sun’s.*
