## In Plain Words

<p align="center"><i>the same ground, for a first reading</i></p>

1. You are allowed to be here.
   You did not have to earn it, and neither did anyone else.

2. The world pushes back.
   That is how you know it is real. What will not move is telling you its shape.

3. When you are handed two choices, look underneath them.
   Ask what question both choices are answering. The better answer is usually to a better question.

4. What you pay attention to grows.
   So place your attention yourself, or someone else will place it for you.

5. Most things are true to a degree.
   Mark how far, and say what would change the mark. Then act in one piece.

6. Be willing to be wrong.
   Say in advance what would change your mind. It is the only way to become more right.

7. See people. Do not run them.
   Stand at the door of another person’s life. Do not move in, and do not let anyone move into yours.

`LIVED` *If you read no further, this page is the book. The levels after it do not add to these lines. They show how to stand on them, how far to trust them, and how they were checked.*
