## The Stance

<p align="center"><i>the whole manual in four lines</i></p>

1. The world has weight.
   Some of it refuses you. That refusal is not an insult; it is the thing that makes attention mean anything.

2. Three is the minimum rich structure.
   Every either/or is a compression someone performed, once, under a constraint. The third is not the midpoint. It is built, never selected.

3. What you attend, you thicken.
   Attention is the one force you spend whether or not you decide to.

4. A claim that cannot lose cannot win.
   This applies to physics. It applies to you.
