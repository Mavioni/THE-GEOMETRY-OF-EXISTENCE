# The Rivers That Became This Book

This book did not begin on its first page. It is the place where many rivers of one man’s work run together, and it keeps what was true in each.

- **Earn Your Reality.** The creed of Peace, Love, Unity and Respect, read as a working philosophy: dignity is the floor, respect is the ceiling.
- **The Enchiridion and the Laws of Earned Power.** The teachings in the voice of a master who fell and came back, and the laws of power that is given rather than taken.
- **Sophonticism and The Geometry of Existence.** The First Principle and its three letters, Existence, Consciousness and Relation, with the rule that mathematics must never pretend to prove a moral truth.
- **Transdifferentiation.** A philosophy of change without erasure: conserved specificity, rejection of the null point, adjacent possibility and non-regressive growth.
- **Reality: The Philosophy.** The meeting of the living cell, relational physics and the practice of holding goals lightly, with each claim marked for what it is.
- **ISOMORPHIC and It From Trit.** The balanced three: refuse, hold, assent. The kept middle, and the search for the third axis.
- **The Forge.** The Stoic architecture of character: what is up to you, the inner citadel, the daily discipline.
- **Speak with Energy.** Language as energy: the voice that carries feeling before it carries meaning.
- **The Adjacent Sight and Learn to Learn.** Perception as reorganization, and skill as patient confrontation with what you cannot yet do.
- **Home Base.** The user’s manual for your own mind: the predicting brain, suggestion and hypnosis, and the dark half of influence with the lights on.
- **Lingua Rotarum and Clavicula Nullius.** The Language of the Wheels: marks, stems and the Kamea of the Sun, a notation for attention built from real mathematics.
- **Vindicated.** The myth of Shatter, and the five stages from Control to Integration: *I don’t have to be God to be myself.*

Every river was tested on the way in. What could not survive the test was left on the bank.
