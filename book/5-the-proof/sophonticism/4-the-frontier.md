## Part IV · The Frontier

### §18 Agency, synthesis and additional degrees of freedom

`ARGUED` A mathematical model of agency can separate four operations: action selection, combination of existing actions, revision of decision rules, and expansion of accessible degrees of freedom. Let A<sub>t</sub> be the agent’s feasible action set, Γ a synthesis operator, R<sub>t</sub> its operative rules and M a rule-update process. Selection takes a<sub>t</sub>∈A<sub>t</sub>. Synthesis proposes c=Γ(a<sub>i</sub>,a<sub>j</sub>) and tests whether c is feasible and absent from the previous menu. Adaptation sets R<sub>t+1</sub>=M(R<sub>t</sub>,H<sub>t</sub>). Dimensional expansion changes the reachable state space rather than merely relabeling coordinates.

The distinction between representational dimension and controllable capacity matters. A line with a deleted midpoint is disconnected; a plane with a deleted origin admits a route around it. That demonstrates a topological opportunity, not a conscious act. An agent still requires a policy that discovers and executes the route. Its objective, data and update mechanisms continue to determine or constrain its behavior.

A possible operational experiment would compare resource-matched agents that can select, synthesize, revise rules and adapt representational capacity. Success criteria would involve out-of-distribution tasks, matched compute, equivalent data access, ablations and independently tested robustness. This could yield a theory of flexible autonomous computation. It would not settle whether the agent has subjective experience or metaphysical free will.

`DEMONSTRATED — PHILOSOPHICAL STATUS` Existing philosophy distinguishes incompatibilist and compatibilist accounts of free will; determinism and agency are not agreed to be mutually exclusive [R9]. Sophonticism cannot bypass that dispute by defining all creative computation as freedom.

**The strongest objection.** A more expressive fixed-dimensional system may emulate any finite dimensional-expansion procedure, making ‘new dimension’ a description rather than an advantage. An operational novelty claim must survive a capacity-matched baseline and avoid defining freedom as whatever the favored agent happens to achieve.

### §19 Quantum gravity, and why an equation-shaped promise is not a theory

`DEMONSTRATED — DOCUMENT AUDIT` The original eight-page *Super Physics* manuscript contains superspace variables, a schematic supermetric, an action symbol, a gravitational path integral, and statements about anomaly cancellation and renormalization. It does **not** specify the complete field content, a consistent component Lagrangian, the gauge group and matter representations, regularization and gauge-fixing conditions, anomaly coefficients, or a discriminating numerical prediction. Those omissions preclude validation of its advertised force unification [S1, S3].

Grassmann coordinates are not automatically ‘quantum dimensions’ in the sense of extra measurable space directions. They are anticommuting coordinates used in supersymmetric formalisms. A symbol ∫DG exp(iS/ℏ) does not by itself define a convergent or regulator-independent quantum theory. A candidate must recover general relativity at tested scales and the Standard Model where the latter applies, preserve acceptable unitarity and symmetries, and say something new that could be checked.

There is a genuine partial bridge: general relativity can be treated as an effective quantum field theory at energies sufficiently below its expected highenergy limitations. It yields systematic low-energy quantum corrections under a controlled expansion [R2]. That does not solve the ultraviolet completion problem. Likewise, geometries involving E8 can appear in certain speculative theoretical constructions, but the lattice in this book is not inferred from gravitational observations.

`ARGUED` Sophonticism may supply a philosophical program for organizing the search—states acquire possible dynamics inside constrained geometries, and distinct descriptions must be reconciled through observables. The program does not currently supply the missing gravitational quantum dynamics.

**The strongest objection.** The entire physics bridge can be constructed from known textbooks and reviews without adopting Sophonticism. It follows that the framework remains an interpretation until it yields a distinct test. Printing one unearned ‘master equation’ would conceal that fact rather than repair it.

### §20 A research program designed to lose

`ARGUED — PROPOSED TEST` The first empirical target should be the six-site geometry comparison of §16, rather than a claim about all cosmic scales. Fix the two graphs in advance; calibrate J, Δ and effective temperature β independently; build or emulate physical three-state units; measure site occupations from repeated equilibrated samples. Blind the data analyst to graph identity if feasible. Fit no new parameters to the outcome after seeing the result.

The stronger, genuinely novel test would require an independently specified extra term δH<sub>sophontic</sub>, with a parameter whose value is predicted without fitting to this dataset, and a difference between its outcome and conventional spin models larger than the instrument’s calibrated uncertainty. **No such term is presently derived.** Calling the absence by its name is more useful than claiming the answer has been found.

A second possible target is the three-body K model of §14. Engineer or independently identify K; test the aligned/opposed occupancy reversal; compare to a K=0 control. This is a sharper test of a specified interaction mechanism but not of new fundamental physics. A third target is hardware: benchmark adaptive ternary versus binary and intelligently fixed-mask controls under matched energy, wall-time, silicon area and accuracy. None of the three targets licenses an inference to free will by itself.

`MEASURED` No new physical dataset has been collected in this edition. The finite enumeration is a checked computation, not a laboratory measurement.

**The strongest objection.** Even a successful physics demonstration would reproduce standard statistical mechanics. The decisive evidence for a new framework would be a *surprising residual* beyond a well-calibrated null model, repeatable on independent devices. We do not yet have that residual or a parameter-free prediction for it.

### §21 the Strongest Case Against

The central weakness is not arithmetic. The arithmetic is elementary and sound once its limits are specified. The weakness is **underdetermination by analogy**: many systems have three states, zero values, gradients, symmetries and network interactions. The ability to describe each using a common word does not imply a common physical substance, a new interaction or a shared causal mechanism.

First, the foundational axiom is philosophically respectable but physically unselective. ‘Existence legitimizes existence’ excludes no Hamiltonian and predicts no scattering result. A critic can accept the axiom and reject every proposed physical extrapolation without contradiction.

Second, the geometric example is an example of established frustration physics. The triangular prism is not equivalent to K₃,₃, and their differing neutral-state occupations follow from the specified spin-1 energy. A conventional physicist can derive the same result without invoking Sophonticism. The existence of a calculable result, by itself, does not establish novelty or fundamental importance.

Third, the assigned-versus-earned-zero narrative is too strong. The zero state in a spin-1 model is installed by its state-space definition and can be thermodynamically consequential from the outset. In the reported neural-network study, a frozen random mask differs from an adaptive mask in optimization opportunities, not merely in an ontological property of ‘generatedness’. There is no theorem that all advance assignment harms every system.

Fourth, higher-dimensional state spaces are not automatically freer. A new coordinate may be gauge redundancy, computational padding or a mathematical embedding. Infinite cardinality can remain unchanged when a dimension is added. Neither E8 nor a fractal exponent is privileged by the observed universe on the evidence presented.

Fifth, quantum field theory and general relativity already possess rigorous, highly constrained empirical domains. The book offers no new quantum-gravity action, gauge completion, anomaly calculation, cosmological likelihood, particle spectrum or measured deviation. It does not and must not present a unified physical theory as accomplished.

Sixth, an agent’s ability to synthesize options and change its rules does not decide the metaphysics of free will. Causal and probabilistic update processes can implement those behaviors. Subjective experience requires a separate theory and evidence. The word ‘sophont’ must not be used to infer consciousness from state-count, symbolic complexity or self-reference.

**Verdict.** As a philosophy of methodological respect for existence and a guide to geometrically constrained possibility, the work may be coherent and useful. As a scientific theory that bridges *all* fundamental interactions, it is unfinished. This is not a rhetorical caveat: it is the strongest available judgment of the supplied mathematics and evidence.

### §22 Conclusion: the right to exist, the obligation to test

`ARGUED` The opening axiom remains where it belongs—before any equation. Whatever exists does not ask a theory’s permission to exist. Our obligation begins afterward, when we attempt to describe it. That obligation is to measure carefully, derive what follows, distinguish it from what we wish would follow, and change our account when reality declines to agree.

Physics already has deep bridges. Action joins force and symmetry; gauge geometry joins local fields and conservation; relativity joins gravity and spacetime; quantum field theory joins quantum principles with relativistic interactions; statistical mechanics joins microscopic states with macroscopic heat; renormalization joins scales and explains some shared long-distance behavior. These connections are not one equation, but they are real and calculable.

The proposed philosophical contribution is a habit of inquiry: do not confuse the options offered by a model with the totality of physical possibility; investigate whether geometry, interaction or additional degrees of freedom permit an unrepresented alternative. Then try to falsify it. The finite graph calculation shows why the habit can matter: systems identical in size and local degree can occupy radically different states because their geometry differs. The same calculation proves why humility is necessary: established physics already explains the result.

Consequence, understood as the effect of recorded outcomes on future transitions, provides a fourth organizing question alongside states, symmetry and scale. The conditional model calculates that question but does not establish a missing fundamental interaction. No secret law is hidden inside a narrator. The answer, when it becomes a physical answer, will be in a reproducible relationship between mathematics and the world. A book cannot summon that relation into evidence. It can state it cleanly enough that nature is allowed to refuse.
