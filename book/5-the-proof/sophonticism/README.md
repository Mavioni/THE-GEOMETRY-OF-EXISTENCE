## Sophonticism

## The Geometry of Existence

<p align="center"><i>A Bridge Across Physics: States, Symmetry, Scale, and Emergent Possibility</i></p>

Research book · first complete working edition · 12 November 2026. Conceptual premise supplied in conversation; mathematical synthesis and drafting prepared with AI assistance.

**Status.** A rigorously bounded synthesis of established physics, a model-derived consequence-memory test, and a new philosophical research program. **Not** a discovered theory of everything, a proof of free will, or an experimentally established new fundamental force.

*The epigraph is the axiom of the book, not its experimental conclusion. Its claim concerns ontological legitimacy. Measurements decide which models describe nature; they do not confer existence upon nature.*

### Reader’s contract: three marks, one standard

`DEMONSTRATED` means mathematically derived under explicit assumptions, computationally exhausted where stated, or accepted established physics with a stated domain. It does **not** mean newly discovered. `MEASURED` means an observed result with specified provenance. `MEASURED — SOURCE-REPORTED` means measurements transcribed from a source without independent rawdata verification. `ARGUED` means a philosophical thesis, interpretive bridge, candidate hypothesis, or extrapolation not settled by derivation or data.

A calculation is not an observation. A representation is not the thing represented. A resemblance is not an isomorphism. A beautiful mathematical object is not, without evidence, the structure of spacetime. Every chapter includes a serious objection: the strongest version of what could make its central inference wrong.

**The governing distinction:** the book attempts to bridge *the descriptions of physics*, not to claim that all interactions have been reduced to one experimentally verified law. Its proposed missing term is **Consequence**—a candidate temporal feedback principle whose mathematical representation is explicit and whose status as a fundamental force is unproved. The Standard Model covers the strong, weak and electromagnetic interactions; a satisfactory full quantum theory of gravity remains outside its established scope [R1, R2].
