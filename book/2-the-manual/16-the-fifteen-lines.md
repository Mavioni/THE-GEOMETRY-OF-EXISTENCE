## The Fifteen Lines

<p align="center"><i>one from each chapter, for returning to</i></p>

1. *A binary with a waiting room is still a binary.*

2. *The ground does not negotiate. That is not its cruelty. That is its honesty.*

3. *You have exactly as much life as you have attention.*

4. *To be seen is not vanity. It is ontology.*

5. *A binary you can see is no longer doing its job.*

6. *The third is built, never selected.*

7. *Insight without position is a discovery made on someone else’s behalf.*

8. *The zero is not the place you escape to. It is the place you return from.*

9. *A decision deferred so long it became the architecture was never deferred. It was made — by default, in secret, against you.*

10. *Seeing the third is cheap and feels like enormous progress. Holding it costs years. Budget for the expensive half or do not begin.*

11. *You are not the same person you were. You are also not a different one. Both facts are load-bearing, and confusing them is how lives break.*

12. *A claim that cannot lose cannot win. This applies to physics. It applies to you.*

13. *Empathy opens a door. It is not a force. What walks through the door is a different thing entirely, and it must be judged on its own.*

14. *Another person’s middle is not yours. Not even to help. Not even to love. Especially not to love.*

15. *The spark has never been destroyed. It has been buried. And the desire to find the spark is the spark itself, flickering.*
