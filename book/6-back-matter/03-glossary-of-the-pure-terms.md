## Glossary of the Pure Terms

**Anchor.** The plain-word meaning given to each end of a graded line, so that someone else could check the mark.

**Asymmetry, the.** Seeing a third is fast and feels like progress. Holding it is slow and feels like none.

**Attention.** The one thing you spend directly and irreversibly. What it rests on, it thickens.

**Binary.** An either/or. Almost always a compression someone performed once, under a constraint. Some are real.

**Catalyst.** What opens a reaction without deciding its product. Empathy is one.

**Channel and water.** The part of a self that survives the loss of every arrangement, and the part that may be revised without betrayal.

**Compression test.** Three questions that tell a manufactured binary from a real one.

**Consequence.** In Level Five, a record of past zero events that shifts the odds of the next state. An engineered feedback, not an established force.

**Crisp act.** One whole deed with a date. The output of graded weighing.

**Degree.** A settled position on a pair’s own line. It undoes the rounding. It is not the third.

**Dignity.** What is owed to whatever exists. The floor. Not graded.

**Falsifier.** A named observation that would count against a claim, committed to before the evidence arrives.

**Given, the.** Whatever refuses your will while remaining real. Not an enemy. Data.

**Ground.** What you stand on. Owned, rented and known, or rented and mistaken for owned.

**Legitimacy.** In the First Principle, the standing a thing has simply by existing.

**Losable claim.** A claim that has agreed to its own test.

**Middle, the.** See *zero.*

**Null.** A deferral with nothing it is waiting on. A waiting room.

**Occupation.** Running another person’s interior from outside: administered attention, claimed naming, claimed veto.

**Respect.** Standing earned by conduct. The ceiling. Graded.

**Reclamation.** The slow return of a buried capacity. Safety, then contact, then patience. A direction, not an arrival.

**Third, the.** A position built underneath a pair, answering the question the pair never asked. Never the midpoint.

**Threshold.** The place between two interiors, each inviolate. The only thing the manual calls sacred.

**Transdifferentiation.** Change that reorganizes what has been built, without passing through a blank.

**Weakest link.** Where several things are required, the lowest mark is the score.

**Witness.** To arrive at another with attention fully placed, take in what is there, and remain.

**Zero, the.** The middle that holds its own weight: the place from which all the layers are reachable. A state a system is in, not a state it is between.
