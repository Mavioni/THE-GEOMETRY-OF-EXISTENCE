## The Graded Audits

<p align="center"><i>One line for each chapter of the manual. Mark it, date it, and mark it again in a month.</i></p>

Each audit below turns a chapter’s question into a line from zero to ten. The anchors are written so that someone else could check them. Where a chapter has more than one requirement, the score is the lowest mark, by the rule of Chapter Seventeen.

Rating lines like these are adapted from techniques used in cognitive and solution-focused therapy. Nothing here is treatment, and no claim is made that a scale used alone, out of a book, has been shown to help anyone. `LIVED` It is an instrument. You will find out what it does by using it.

**1 · The Three and the Zero.** *The line:* how often I see a pair as a pair. *Zero:* I pick a side at once. *Ten:* I write the pair as one sentence and ask what question it answers before I choose. *It moves when* I count the either/ors I wrote down this week.

**2 · The Given.** *The line:* how plainly I can state what refuses me. *Zero:* only with complaint or denial. *Ten:* one flat sentence, no adjectives, said aloud. *It moves when* I write the sentence and can read it to someone.

**3 · Attention.** *The line:* how much of today’s attention I placed on purpose. *Zero:* all of it was taken. *Ten:* all of it was placed. *It moves when* I keep one evening’s honest log of where it went.

**4 · Witness.** *The line:* how long I can stay with someone’s pain before I reach for a fix. *Zero:* I fix at once. *Ten:* five full minutes. *It moves when* I time it, once, this week.

**5 · Name the Binary.** *The line:* how examined my loudest either/or is. *Zero:* never written down. *Ten:* written as one sentence, premise found, all three test questions answered on paper.

**6 · Construct the Third.** *Two lines:* what my third cost me from the first pole, and from the second. *Zero:* nothing. *Ten:* a real loss I can name. *The score is the lower.* A third that cost nothing from one side is a compromise with a better name.

**7 · Own the Ground.** *The line:* for each load-bearing wall, how far the floor under it is mine. *Zero:* revocable by someone else tomorrow. *Ten:* it would take my own cooperation to lose it. *The score is the shakiest floor.*

**8 · Keep the Middle.** *The line:* days this week I reached all three layers from the middle at least once. Count them. Seven days is ten.

**9 · Refuse the Waiting Room.** *The line:* how many of my deferrals are waiting on something I can name. *Zero:* none has a date or a condition. *Ten:* every one does, or has its certificate.

**10 · The Asymmetry.** *Four lines:* years, comfort, company, evidence — is each cost written down? *The score is the least-priced cost.* A construction priced on three sides fails on the fourth.

**11 · Continuity.** *The line:* how clearly I can name what survives the stripping. *Zero:* I am my arrangements. *Ten:* one written line, and my last revision was judged against it.

**12 · The Losable Claim.** *The line:* how losable my chief claim about myself is. *Zero:* nothing could count against it. *Ten:* a written falsifier, committed in advance, and one witness knows it.

**13 · Empathy as Catalyst.** *Two lines:* accuracy — would they sign my statement of their position? — and welfare — did I do what their situation required? *The score is the lower.*

**14 · The Inviolate.** *Three lines, each run both ways:* administered attention, claimed naming, claimed veto. *Zero:* constant. *Ten:* absent. *The score is the worst of the three,* once for what I do and once for what is done to me.

**15 · The Long Reclamation.** *The line:* days this week I asked one small question and let the answer be small. Count them. There is no ten to reach here. There is only the next day.
