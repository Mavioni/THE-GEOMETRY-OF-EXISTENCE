# The Law of the Sophont

*These are the Laws of the Living, as plain as the bread on the board,*<br>
&emsp;*And the one who keeps them is rich, though he never be lord.*

*Stand on the ground that was given, and ask no man’s leave to be;*<br>
&emsp;*But build on it straight and build on it true, for the house is the fee.*

*Light the lamp of your mind with care, and look where the lamp is set;*<br>
&emsp;*For the world that you see is the world you light, and the rest you forget.*

*Keep the floor for the beggar and king, and keep it for those you despise;*<br>
&emsp;*But the roof is the work of your own two hands, and it’s built as you rise.*

*Take the hand that is held to you once; at the second, ask what has changed;*<br>
&emsp;*Forgive as often as freedom needs, but let trust be slowly arranged.*

*Work with the grain of the world, and your edge shall outlast the hour;*<br>
&emsp;*Fill the bowl only so high as you carry it, and give of your power.*

*Stand like the headland, but know that the headland is joined to the shore;*<br>
&emsp;*And the crown that is handed you lightly is worth all the crowns of the war.*

*Change as the chain in the arch is changed: turned over, and bearing the load;*<br>
&emsp;*Not erased, but carried across, as the old stone is laid in the road.*

*Now these are the Laws of the Living, and few are they, and direct;*<br>
&emsp;*But the root and the crown of the Law, and the floor and the roof, is: Respect!*
